It does not establish that the many independently accepted contracts and implementations form one eligible physical
qualification candidate. LIVE-280 freezes that candidate boundary before any assembler, owner authorization, or native
effect can exist.

The contract must give Control Room one unambiguous answer to three questions:

1. Which exact accepted repository artifacts must a future candidate bind?

3. Which operations remain impossible until a later candidate assembler and fresh owner-attended authorization are
